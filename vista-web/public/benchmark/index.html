METHOD

One-to-one event matching

Predicted and reviewed events must agree on type, direction, optional vehicle class, and timestamp within ±1 second. Each event can match once.

LIMIT

Short, single-reviewer sample

Eleven crossings across 50 seconds of footage do not establish performance in new light, weather, road layouts, or heavy occlusion. No real stop events were manually labeled.
